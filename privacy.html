Privacy Policy

PromptGen AI · Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short

PromptGen AI has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your favorites and settings stay on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and can send you notifications through Google Firebase, and Google collects some device data to do both. You can turn notifications off at any time. This page explains exactly what that means.

1. Who we are

PromptGen AI ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Himanshu Jangir. You can contact us at himanshu.developer123@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect or store on our own servers your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or your precise location. We do not read your clipboard. When you tap "Copy prompt", the app only writes the prompt text to your clipboard.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores a few things locally on your phone so that it works offline and remembers your choices:

  • the prompts and categories downloaded for the app;
  • which prompts you marked as favorites and when you last used a prompt;
  • small settings, such as whether you have seen our notice, your notification choices, how long ads are paused after you watch a rewarded video, and reminder times for app updates and review requests.

This information is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

4. Information collected automatically by third parties

Google AdMob (ads). The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, to analyze performance and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and operating system details, app interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information (such as launch time and hang rate), and information about the ads you see or tap. We do not receive this data from Google in a form that identifies you. Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications). To tell you when new prompts are added, the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, a service provided by Google. To deliver a notification to your device, Firebase processes a Firebase installation ID and a push token for your device. We send notifications to all subscribers of a topic, so we do not see or store individual tokens ourselves. See Firebase privacy information.

Daily reminder. The optional daily reminder is created on your device by the app. It does not send any data to us or to a third party.

Content downloads. To keep the prompt list up to date, the app downloads a file with prompts and images from a server or hosting service we use. Like any web request, this lets that hosting service see your IP address and basic technical details such as your device's user agent. We do not use this to identify you.

Google Play. If you installed the app from Google Play, the in-app update and in-app review features are provided by Google Play. Google handles that data under its own policies. We only receive the result needed to run the feature (for example, that an update is available).

5. Notifications, ads, consent and your choices

  • Notifications. The app can send two kinds of notifications: alerts when new prompts are added, and a daily reminder. You can turn each one off in the app under About → Notifications, or in your phone's notification settings. Turning off an alert stops us from sending it to your device.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice later in the app under About → Ad privacy settings, when that option is shown.
  • United States. If you live in a US state with a privacy law that gives you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising, the app shows a message where you can make that choice. You can change it later in the app under About → Ad privacy settings.
  • On your device. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalization in your phone's settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Ad-free time. You can choose to watch a short rewarded video to pause ads for a limited time. This is optional.

6. How we use and share information

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, we do not sell it, rent it or use it for profiling. The only sharing that happens is the data Google AdMob collects directly from your device for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and the data Firebase Cloud Messaging processes to deliver notifications, both as described in section 4.

7. Children

PromptGen AI is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep no personal data about you on our servers. To remove everything the app stores on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Uninstalling the app also stops notifications and ends the Firebase installation for your device. To manage data held by Google, use the controls in your Google Account and your device settings. You can also email us to ask about any data linked to notifications or ads and we will respond.

9. Security

Downloads and data sent to Google services use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but because we hold no personal data about you, there is very little for us to protect.

10. International transfers

Google operates globally, so ad-related and notification-related data may be processed in countries other than your own. Google describes the safeguards it uses in its privacy policy.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information, and to object to its use. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete, but you can contact us with any request or question and we will respond. For ad-related and notification-related data held by Google, you can use Google's privacy tools. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, show a notice in the app. This update (16 October 2026) added information about notifications and expanded the description of ad-related data.
