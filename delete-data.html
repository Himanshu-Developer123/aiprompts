Data Deletion Request

PromptGen AI · Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short

PromptGen AI has no accounts and does not store personal data about you on our servers. Almost everything the app saves is on your own phone, and you can remove it yourself in a minute. You can also email us to ask about any data linked to notifications or ads, and we will respond.

1. Delete data stored on your device

Your favorites, copied-prompt history and settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. To remove them:

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → PromptGen AI → Storage.
  2. Tap Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Uninstalling also stops all notifications and ends the Firebase installation for your device.

2. Stop notifications

In the app, open About → Notifications and switch off new-prompt alerts and the daily reminder.

3. Ad data held by Google

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and the data it collects (such as your advertising ID) is held by Google, not by us. To manage it:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (the path varies by phone).
  • Manage ad personalization and data in your Google Ad Center and Google Account.

4. Send us a deletion request

If you want us to look into any data linked to you, email himanshu.developer123@gmail.com with the subject "PromptGen AI data deletion request". Please include the device model and Android version so we can help. We will reply within 30 days.

5. What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted on request: any data we hold that can be linked to your device or to you.
  • Not held by us: favorites, history and settings (they live only on your phone), and ad identifiers (held by Google).
  • Kept: nothing that identifies you. We do not keep personal data on our servers.

See also our Privacy Policy.
